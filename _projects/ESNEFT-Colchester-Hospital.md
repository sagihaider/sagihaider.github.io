* Role: Principal Investigator (PI) <br/>
* Duration: December 2018-March 2019 <br/>

**Project Summary**

This project investigated artificial intelligence as a means of supporting decision-making in a hospital setting. It focused on the potential for data-driven methods to assist healthcare professionals and improve the use of information within clinical and operational processes.
