* Duration: 30 months, from August 2026 <br/>

**Project Summary**

East AI Studio is an accelerated skills and transformation initiative focused on generative and agentic artificial intelligence. The project supports the development and responsible application of emerging AI capabilities while strengthening the knowledge and skills needed to translate them into practice.
