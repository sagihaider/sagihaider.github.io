I am delighted to be named among **Universities UK's 50 Future Innovators** in the **Health and Medicine** category, recognising my work developing AI tools for earlier skin cancer detection.

![Dr Haider Raza, University of Essex, recognised among the 50 Future Innovators for developing AI tools for earlier skin cancer detection]({{ '/assets/img/UUK_Award_2026.jpg' | relative_url }})

The recognition highlights the AI behind **SKINTEL®**, which analyses an image of a suspicious skin mark alongside information such as age, skin tone and family history, and explains its reasoning to clinicians.

50 Future Innovators is a UK-wide celebration of people whose work has the potential to change lives. Independent expert judges selected the 50 innovators from more than 300 nominations, considering the originality and timeliness of their work, its potential impact, and the role of a UK university in making it possible.

As I shared in the campaign: “My university journey provided me with the curiosity, confidence, and leadership skills to tackle real-world challenges through AI and digital innovation.”
